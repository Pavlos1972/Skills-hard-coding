# life-insurer-comparison: Phase A findings (Operation Skill Restructure, 04/11/2026)

Read-only analysis of the LIVE skill (Paul's synced copy, 14/10/2026 15:14 build). **No client data is in the live file** (checked against the private names list: no hit; no ID number, phone or email either; no Rand amounts at all, only "R0" and "R[amount]" placeholders), so the archive `SKILL.2026-10-14.md` is the live text unchanged (same checksum). ## Facts
- Live skill: ONE file, `SKILL.md`, 1,076 lines (70,105 bytes). **No script, no template, no asset, no reference page.** The baseline is 956 single rules (sentences) in `SKILL.2026-10-14.md`.
- Job: compare two or more insurers' life/risk policies (any number of existing policies and quotes, e.g. BrightRock against Discovery) and build a client-facing HTML letter from Paul: Header, Key Facts Banner, salutation, up to five ticked sections (Premium Snapshot, Cover Comparison, Premium Escalation Table, Loyalty/Payback table, Definition Quality), Summary/Verdict, signed closing, Disclaimer/Sources; plus a covering email.
- Receives from: `client-pipeline-board` (its Life Cover playbook: "Insurer Comparison (replacement scenarios)": Paul is asked which approach, like-for-like or against the new proposal, the moment the current schedule lands; the comparison must be ready before the 2nd appointment), the client's quotes (Risk Transaction / Comparative Quotes folder) and in-force policy schedules, the intake notes (Follow-Up Appointment Summary Notes, Fact Find Summary, WR Overview). Hands over to: Paul, in the chat (the text never says how: flaw 2). Related: `life-policy-schedule-analyzer` (one insurer against itself; points here for replacements), `investment-comparisons-skill` (borrowed this skill's look and tone).
- Shape: 9 universal operating rules and a 5-item Delivery gate, then Steps 0, 0a, 0b (scope gate), 1 (retrieve), 2 (product research), 3 (verify facts), 4 (premium pattern), 5 (accuracy, escalation check, OCR), 6 (loyalty table), 7 (unique features), 8 (definitions), 9 (second life), 10 (format and design), 11 (what not to do), 12 (pre-delivery checks), Companion email.

## Flaws found
Contradictions and gaps:
1. **The skill asks before it looks (S3).** Step 0 asks three questions (which insurers, second life, quote or schedule), Step 0a two more (the section tick-box and the voice), Step 0b may ask two (scope), Step 1 asks to confirm the files: six to nine separate touches before any work, most of it readable from the client's folder (the quotes, the schedules, the second life, the scope notes).
2. **The hand-over is never specified.** The Delivery gate says the document is "handed over the way Step 10 specifies"; Step 10 only says "a standalone HTML file ... convertible to PDF on request". No folder, no file name, no save, no listing back, no overwrite rule. (S6)
3. **The board expects more than the skill delivers.** The board's other product lines end with an Outlook draft (To the client, Cc Sitamah) and a Teams note to Sitamah; this skill ends with "email drafted and shown in the chat". The board's Actions Taken line also wants the basis ("like-for-like / against-proposal"), which the skill never asks or records.
4. **A rule points at a number nobody wrote.** Step 4 and Step 5 (lines 487, 574) say a figure that looks high or low "against Paul's own stated industry baseline" must be investigated. The baseline is not in the skill anywhere.
5. **"No insurer facts hardcoded" is contradicted by Step 2's trap list.** Scope says "no insurer's product facts are ever hardcoded here"; Step 2 then hardcodes Discovery's ACI/ABI, BrightRock's fixed ACI, Momentum's flat Compulsory/Voluntary/Total and Liberty's "Age Rated Premium Pattern". These can go stale. Also the palette text (Step 10) says "by position, never by brand" and then says the lead insurer is "typically BrightRock" with `--ins-1` accent: the dark lead card is tied to slot 1, not to whichever insurer is the proposal.
6. **Step 5 versus Steps 2 and 7 on outside knowledge.** Step 5: a figure that cannot be verified "must not appear". Steps 2 and 7: general product knowledge from the insurer's public materials may appear if flagged. The line between a client figure (never from outside) and a product feature (may be, flagged) is never drawn.
7. **The scope check word list is too short.** Step 12 greps five terms (severe illness, dread disease, critical illness, lump sum disability, income protection). Step 0b's categories also include debt cover, childcare/education, and plain "disability"; none are scanned. Terminal illness is always in scope (Step 3) but the grep cannot tell.
8. **Row or column?** Step 4 says each insurer's "row" carries the year-on-year badge; Step 10 makes each insurer a column. Minor, but a template must choose.
9. **Order is circular.** Step 0b must run "before retrieving quotes" yet needs the intake documents Step 1 retrieves ("or in the same pass"); Step 11 says do not build until 0, 0a, 0b and 1 are done. Works only by reading the text twice.
10. **Out of date:** intake notes are said to "typically live in the client's main folder"; the skills that make them now save them in `2. Fact Find`; the old text predates that. Also out of date: the `/mnt/project/...` file commands (a claude.ai Project path), "device bridge", "propose_skills" (rule 9), "re-load this skill" (rule 1), a hand-typed "Last updated" and changelog (S2), and "older entries are in `life-insurer-comparison-changelog.md` in the Project" (a file I cannot see; it is not in the repo or the zip).
11. **Access routes (S4):** Step 1 names a synced local folder or the Microsoft 365 connector and, if neither works, "ask Paul to connect one". Zapier is not tried; nothing is logged.
12. **Dates and money (S13):** the old text never says DD/MM/YYYY or how Rand amounts are written.
13. **The closing has no contact details.** Step 10 and the Companion email want phone and email, "leave as clearly-marked placeholders if not provided": a placeholder can reach the client.
14. **A leftover placeholder in the check:** Step 12's personalisation grep still contains the literal `<ClientName>` (left by the 14/10 redaction), which can never match.
15. **Fonts** come from Google Fonts only, with no fallback stack, so a document opened offline or in a locked-down browser silently changes look.
Nothing forces the rules (text only):
16. **Every figure is typed by the chat** from a quote or schedule; nothing proves it is in the document, that the total is the document's own total, that the year-on-year percentages were recomputed, or that the escalation figure "recomputed from source" was.
17. **Fixed versus age-rated** (repeated 22 times) must come from the document's "literal wording"; nothing records or checks that wording.
18. **Step 12 is eleven checks done by eye with grep lines** (scope, escalation, Section 4 simplicity, letter format, personalisation, structure, section selection, % badges, quote-version history, honesty). Most are checkable by a program; the word lists (version history: "removed", "previous") need a human look at each hit.
19. **The "who wins" badges, the "who's ahead" assessment, the Key Facts Banner and the Verdict are judgement:** no way to show Paul exactly what will be claimed before it is built.
20. **Scanned schedules:** Step 1 and Step 5 spend a lot of text on image-only PDFs, `tesseract`, crop and upscale, per-page rotation, Claude in Chrome. A figure read from an image cannot be proved against text.
21. **No access log, run state, reading lock, step driver, checker, tests, package (S4, S8, S12).**
Clutter (S5):
22. **Duplicated text:** the scope rule is repeated 24 times ("Step 0b"), fixed-versus-age-rated 22, the voice rule 18, the minimal-table rule (Section 4) 14, OCR 12, the Overview-versus-quote rule 7 (Step 1 and Step 5), the salutation/closing rule 8. Measured: the Index, the nine copied universal rules, the Delivery gate, the typed stamp and the changelog are 52 lines, and Step 11 (60 lines) only restates earlier steps: 112 lines, about 10%, before counting the repeats inside Steps 2 to 10 and 12. The Honesty check (Step 12) restates Step 5 point 4.

## What a program CAN and CANNOT do here (honest)
- CAN: search the client's folder first and log every route (computer, Microsoft 365, Zapier); serve each document page by page in order and refuse to move on until each is acknowledged; show ONE confirmation table (insurers, quote or schedule, second life, scope with the exact lines from the intake notes that support it, default sections, voice, basis, file names) and refuse to build without Paul's yes; check every figure (premiums, rates, covers, guarantee periods, projection years, loyalty percentages, dates, reference numbers, names) against the right document's text next to its label; recompute every year-on-year percentage and compare it with any printed rate; find which projection row equals the "current premium" (anchor check); flag outliers by comparison with the same insurer's neighbouring years and the other insurers; require the premium-pattern wording as a quotation and prove the quotation is in the document; build the HTML from one fixed template (positional colours, fallback fonts, `.na` cells, a badge on every row that has a figure, "printed" or "calculated" footnote, sticky columns for four or more insurers, salutation after the banner, closing before the disclaimer); scan the finished HTML and the email for every out-of-scope category word, for third-person slips and the banned phrases, for "previous/no longer" version-history words (a human looks at each hit), for broken tables and orphaned footnotes; list the Sources from the documents' own registry; build the tiered honesty answer from labels each statement carries (client document, insurer guide, inference); derive file names, never overwrite without Paul's yes; read the Outlook draft and the Teams message back; print the short audit.
- CANNOT: do the product research on the web or prove the saved text is the real, current page; judge whether a feature is really exclusive to one insurer, whether a definition is better, who wins, or whether the Verdict is fair; decide the scope (it can only show the evidence); prove an image-only figure (those go on the "only attested" line with the page number); prove the prose is plain, warm and natural; attach the HTML to the Outlook draft from here (unproven) or see Outlook and Teams themselves; read a layout it has never seen. **I have never seen a real BrightRock, Discovery, Momentum, Liberty or Old Mutual schedule or quote**, so the first real run may need one fix.
- Size: about twice the work of `investment-comparisons-skill` (956 rules to account for; many are repeats of the same few rules, listed above). One pass in this chat, no helpers.
