You never write the board. You write ONE file, `change.json`, with what changed in this run; the program applies it to the old board and checks every rule.

```
{"ops": [ ... ], "emails": [ ... ], "notify": [ ... ], "fyi": true}
```

**ops** (in this order of need):
- `{"op":"init","client":{"first":"..","surname":".."},"kind":"prospect|existing","paul_said":"his answer"}` the first run only.
- `{"op":"add_line","line":"life_cover|retirement_annuity|investments|rgar|egar|medical_aid|will|short_term"}` a product line is created from the Fact Find's real values, or later when Paul reports a new need.
- `{"op":"tag","line":"life_cover","tag":"New Business|Servicing","paul_said":".."}` for Life Cover, RA and Investments only, the moment the line is created. Ask Paul directly: "Is this a new policy or product, or a servicing change to something [Client] already has?" Never infer it from documents. RGAR, EGAR, Medical Aid, Will and Short-Term never carry the tag. The tag decides the quote types and the document the line ends in (`wr-roa-builder` or `wr-sroa-builder`). A Servicing line starts from a fresh, full Fact Find.
- `{"op":"identified","card":"fact_find|general|<line>","bullets":[{"text":"..","evidence":{"doc":"fact_find_summary","line":"a verbatim line of that document"}} or {"text":"..","paul_said":".."}, optional "provisional":"why"]}` REPLACES that card's Identified list (it is the current state). Short point form, at most 25 words, one fact, no commentary, no stage codes. Every figure in a bullet must be in its evidence line.
- `{"op":"pending","card":"general|<line>","text":"..?"}` and `{"op":"pending_clear","card":".."}` the single question awaiting Paul, plain text, at most 70 words, ending in a question mark. Never on the Fact Find card.
- `{"op":"action","card":"general|<line>","text":"..","receipt":"KEY","via":"<skill>"}` what Claude actually produced or sent, one fact per bullet (at most 30 words). The receipt key must already be recorded (`run.py receipt`); `via` names a product skill the work was handed to. Actions Taken is always Claude's own action and never restates an Identified fact.
- `{"op":"followup","date":"DD/MM/YYYY","time":"HH:MM","source":"..","via":"teams-meeting-setup-skill","receipt":"KEY"}` the confirmed follow-up or 2nd appointment (South African time). The page shows it SA time first with the Greek time in brackets, for Paul only. A move adds `"reschedule":true` and needs the receipt `checkin.deleted`; the existing invite is updated in place, never recreated.
- `{"op":"prospect_log","date":"DD/MM/YYYY","text":".."}` the short contact and booking log.
- `{"op":"close","status":"NTU|Inactive","paul_said":".."}` see Close-out below.

**Cards.** *Fact Find*: Identified only (what the form actually says, product by product, plus the confirmed follow-up date). No pending question, no Actions Taken. *General* (client-level): the one section that talks to the client about documents; its Identified list is exactly the documents WR needs from the client, gathered once across every product for one combined email, reconciled against the on-file check. Pending is the single question "Ready to send [Client] one email for [documents]?"; when Paul says yes, in the same reply draft the consolidated email (To the client, Cc Sitamah, a plain list of documents, no typed sign-off) and save it to Outlook Drafts. The LOA signature request is NOT part of it. *Product lines*: General is client-facing only; product cards are provider-facing only: a product card never asks the client for a document, only the insurer or provider (quote, report, referral, application, proposal). A document specific to one product still goes through General if it is client-facing. A Claude-drafted proposal and the RGAR request are auto lines: no pending question, straight to Actions Taken. A new line gets the New Business / Servicing question immediately.

**The pending question.** For Life Cover it is ONE combined question covering everything answerable right now (which insurer for the FNA, and, if Group Risk is unclear, whether to proceed with a Reduced Quote now on an FNA basis and with which insurer). The Shortfall question is genuinely later, once the Group Risk Benefit Statement is back.

**Follow-up appointment.** The moment a follow-up (2nd or later) appointment is confirmed, from the transcript or from Paul, record it with `followup` and hand over to `teams-meeting-setup-skill`: it builds the invite, shows it to Paul, and sends it only after his yes (the client is emailed when it is created). Client meetings are Teams meetings only. Times are always SA time first; never mention Paul's location to a client and never put a bracketed Greek time in anything a client sees. The same-day check-in is a scheduled task the program prints the exact settings for (`run.py checkin`). One follow-up appointment can cover several lines; once the transcript is uploaded run `client-teams-transcript-skill` and read it against every open line. There is no fixed outcome list: read what happened, then ask the specific follow-up question or take the next permitted action.

**Close-out.** Most clients never need it: an Active client's board stays open. Close-out is only for NTU (declined everything at prospect stage, no policy placed) and Inactive (the relationship has ended). Never auto-close: when every open line is resolved, ask Paul once: "Every line here is resolved. Is [Client] fully closed out (NTU or Inactive), or should the board stay open?" If he says stay open, do not ask again until something changes materially. When he confirms: cancel the client's scheduled check-ins first (record `closeout.triggers_cancelled`), enter `close`; the page then shows "CLOSED - [status] - [date]" at the top, the Change Log gets its final entry, and the page is republished. Never delete the chat or the board. The cross-client board index is not updated by this program (its location is not known): the audit says so.

**Emails** (each an object): `id`, `route` (from the send table), `card`, `to`, `cc`, `subject`, `body_html`, `action_text` (the Actions Taken line once it is done), `asked_paul` (his words, where the table says ask first), `thread` (`reply_all_to` a message id, or `none_found` with what you searched), `client_details` (name, ID number, cell, email, and for each the registered document it comes from, in `evidence`) for the Medical Aid and Will referrals, and `attach` / `revised_quote` where they apply. See `03-route.md`.

**notify**: the Sitamah messages that are due: `{"trigger":"general_email_drafted","slots":{"draft_subject":".."}}` (see `04-send.md` for the list). The one FYI message is built by the program from this run's ordinary Actions Taken, except those a product skill already told her about itself.
