### Life Cover
| Internal stage | What happens | Feeds the board as |
|---|---|---|
| Info Gathering | LOA explanatory email, then LOA+Disclosure via MSIGN (gate: no Astute report until signed LOA back). once the LOA is signed and saved to the client's OneDrive folder, Claude does not draw the Astute report itself: it messages Sitamah (Action needed) that the LOA is signed and saved, and asks her to draw the Astute report herself. Client documents needed: current personal risk policy schedule(s) if existing cover, Group Risk Benefit Statement if GR confirmed or unclear, latest POI. | These document needs go on **General**, not this card. |
| Quote Prep | **New Business — ask everything answerable right now in ONE message, not one question at a time:** (1) which insurer for the FNA quote (standard list, BrightRock first: BrightRock, Discovery Life, Sanlam, Momentum, Old Mutual — skip if the FF already names one), and (2) if Group Risk is still unclear, whether Paul wants a Reduced Quote now on an FNA basis or wants to hold for GR confirmation — and if now, which insurer for it (don't assume it matches the FNA insurer; ask). Neither answer depends on the other, so both go in the same chat message. **The **Shortfall** question is genuinely separate in time, not just bundled differently** — it can only be asked once the GR Benefit Statement actually comes back confirming GR present, so it stays its own later question on its own: which insurer for the Shortfall Quote (GR present → Reduced re-based on Shortfall; if a Reduced-on-FNA-basis request already went out, flag under Identified that it needs re-basing). If GR comes back confirmed absent, no Shortfall question. Standard quote from another insurer only if the FF names one outright — no question needed. **Servicing:** ask, in one message, both which combination applies (Servicing FNA always; Shortfall only if GR cover exists; Alternative only if affordability constrains below Shortfall/FNA) and which insurer — answerable together, not sequentially. Each drafted quote-request email CCs Sitamah. **When the insurer is BrightRock/Lizelle specifically, the quote request needs the client's Fact Find Summary PDF attached** — confirmed 2026-09-04: build the email exactly as before, then insert the line `Please see attached Fact Find Summary for this client.` immediately before the closing urgency line ("Please could you get this back to me as soon as possible" or its equivalent), and save it to Outlook Drafts as normal (this line stays a genuine draft — it is not one of the direct-send lines). In the same reply, message Sitamah on Teams asking her to open the draft, attach the client's Fact Find Summary PDF from their OneDrive folder, send it to Lizelle herself, **and follow up with Lizelle for the quotes back** *(added 2026-09-16)* — see "Keeping Sitamah in the loop" → "When to message her" for the wording (this is an action request, not the usual FYI). | Life Cover card: Identified lists the quote(s) needed and any confirmed insurer; pendingQueue asks one combined question covering everything currently answerable, with the Shortfall question as its own separate later item once the GR statement is back; Actions Taken logs the request once sent, and — for a Lizelle request — logs that Sitamah was asked to attach and send. |
| Presentation | Present Overview + quotes at the follow-up appointment. Client decides. | Pending: "What did he decide — accept, decline, needs more time, or another meeting?" Log the answer and route accordingly. See "Follow-up appointment tracking" above. |
| Application Prep (New Business) | **Pre-filled automatically, ahead of the 2nd/3rd meeting**, once enough is available from the quote, FF, and any proposal (`complete-applications-skill`, General Short Risk Application Template) — not gated on formal Acceptance. Fills what it can (address, banking, beneficiaries, COC date/time, DOC, replacement Y/N, RAR Y/N where known); flags plainly what's still missing for Paul to complete live. What's Next email, MSIGN signing pack, and COC invite still follow once the client actually accepts. FICA (48hr follow-up email, 72hr call, notify Paul at 5 days outstanding). | Actions Taken logs the pre-fill as its own bullet ("Risk application pre-filled — missing: [items]"), then each subsequent step. |
| Insurer Comparison (replacement scenarios) | Not a silent auto-build: it needs Paul's input, and timing is critical. The moment the client's current policy schedule is saved to their OneDrive folder, ask Paul directly which comparison approach to take: (a) request a fresh like-for-like quote from the client's current insurer to compare directly against their existing policy, or (b) compare the new insurer's proposal (e.g. a BrightRock Reduced Quote) directly against the client's current policy. **The comparison must be ready before the 2nd/follow-up appointment** — this is exactly why getting the client's current policy schedule onto General's ask early matters so much; the like-for-like path especially needs lead time to request and receive its own quote. Once Paul answers, Claude requests whatever's still needed and builds the comparison via `life-insurer-comparison` as soon as both sides are in hand. Any number of insurers; for RA and Investments see `investment-comparisons-skill`. | Actions Taken: "Insurer comparison built ([existing insurer] vs [new insurer], [like-for-like/against-proposal] basis)." |
| COC | Several steps. Claude creates and sends the COC appointment invite (fixed 30-minute duration), then messages Sitamah (Action needed) that it's been sent, asking her to watch for acceptance. If still not accepted 2 days before the scheduled date, flag Paul to call the client. On the day of the COC appointment itself, message Sitamah (Action needed) asking her to call the client as a reminder. Client completes the COC call with BrightRock. **One hour after the appointment's scheduled time, message Sitamah (Action needed) asking her to confirm whether it was actually completed** — only once she confirms, send the email requesting the tele-underwriting transcript. Once FICA + signed application + confirmed COC are all in, compile the submission to Lizelle using the existing Outlook template Sitamah already uses for new application/servicing submissions (Template library convention — see Ground rules). | Actions Taken: "COC invite sent, Sitamah asked to watch for acceptance.", "COC completed, confirmed by Sitamah.", "Submission sent to Lizelle." |
| Underwriting | **Standard Acceptance** → moves straight to Policy Issue. **Requirements Issued** → sub-track (PMA/GP/nurse/blood/GPMR). Once every requirement is submitted to the insurer, they underwrite the case on their own; Claude/Sitamah does not notify them the file is complete. Just monitor for their decision. **RAR Process (replacement cases only):** Claude compiles the RAR request email to the insurer and saves it to Outlook Drafts, then messages Sitamah (Action needed) that an RAR is required for this client/product, the email is drafted, and asks her to check it, attach the policy schedule(s) of the policy being replaced, and send it — she then monitors for the completed RAR to come back. Once it's back: save it, send it to the client for signature via MSIGN, follow up if not signed within 48 hours, then submit the signed RAR to the insurer. **Counter Offer Letter — full sequence:** (1) request a written reason for the COL from the insurer; (2) once the reason is in, ask Paul whether he agrees with it; (3) once he does, prepare WR's own client-facing COL communication and send it to the client; (4) Paul calls the client to discuss; (5) client accepts or declines; (6) if the client accepts, they must reply to the email confirming acceptance in writing; (7) only once that written confirmation is in, send a COL MSIGN pack for the client to electronically sign the actual counter-offer documents; (8) submit the signed documents to the insurer; (9) the policy is then issued. | Pending, once the insurer's reason for the COL is in: "[Insurer]'s stated reason for the counter offer is [X] — do you agree with it?" Once Paul agrees and the client-facing COL is sent: nothing further until Paul reports the call outcome. Actions Taken logs each step as it completes. |
| Policy Issue | Welcome Letter + policy docs. **New Business → `wr-roa-builder`; Servicing → `wr-sroa-builder`** — see "ROA vs SROA" below. Welcome Email. Paul confirms in force with client. Cancellation Letter to previous insurer only now, if replacement. | Actions Taken: "Policy issued, Welcome Email sent.", "[ROA/SROA] completed and saved." |
| Compliance (mandatory 17-point gate) | Audit: FF, Astute report, all previous policy schedules incl. GR/pension/provident, signed LOA, signed Disclosure, PDF Overview, client-signed quote, client-signed BR application, signed Cancellation Letter (if replacement), signed COL (if issued), signed RAR (if replacement+RAR), signed ROA/SROA, FICA (ID/utility bill/bank confirmation/POI), Risk Tolerance Questionnaire (investment cases only, N/A here). Update client record. Next Review Date = +365 days. | Actions Taken: "Compliance audit complete." Flag to Paul only if an item is missing. |
