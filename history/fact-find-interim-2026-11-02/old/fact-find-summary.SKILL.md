## Mandatory: transcript accuracy — no mistakes allowed

Every field populated from the Audio to Text file or a meeting transcript must be verified against those sources before it is treated as final. This is a hard requirement, not a best-effort guideline — a transcript-derived mistake in a Fact Find is a compliance problem, not just a formatting one.

- **Check against the Audio to Text file first, then the transcript (rule 10).** Re-open the client's `Fact Find (Audio to Text)` PDF and JSON and the Teams transcript and check the Fact Find's values against both, not just against the Fact Find's own text. Where the two differ, the Audio to Text value is the one used and the difference stays flagged with both values; never silently switch to the transcript's value and never drop the flag. Whatever is populated in the Audio to Text file (confirmed, heard, verify or preset) is a populated value and belongs in the Summary.
- **"Not discussed" is a claim, not a default.** A field that was actually discussed and answered on the call, or populated in the Audio to Text file, must never be left as "Not discussed", a guessed figure, or a stale value carried over from an old document. Before marking anything "Not discussed", confirm that by actually re-checking the Audio to Text file and the transcript for it — don't use it as a safe fallback when you're simply unsure.
- **Re-search, don't skim.** Before finalizing any section built from a transcript, re-search the transcript for every relevant keyword/topic (not just the passage you remember from a first pass) and read the full surrounding exchange, not just the one sentence that looks like the answer. Numbers in particular are often corrected or restated later in the same call.
- **Use the corrected figure, and say so.** Where a figure was corrected live on the call (the client restates a number, or the advisor recaps a different one), use the final/corrected figure as the field value, and note the correction and the original figure in that field's Notes so the advisor can see the discrepancy at a glance. (If the Audio to Text file holds a different value from the corrected one, the Audio to Text value is used and the difference flagged, per the first bullet.)
- **Flag genuine ambiguity — don't silently resolve it either way.** Where a figure is genuinely unclear on the recording (two plausible readings, a number that doesn't match a restatement elsewhere in the call), flag it plainly with a ⚠ note stating both candidate values. Don't silently pick one, and don't silently mark it "Not discussed" to avoid the ambiguity — either is a mistake.
- **This is the checkpoint that reviews the whole Fact Find, not just this skill's own output.** Because this skill typically runs last in the chain (after `ff-population-transcript` has built the Fact Find itself), treat producing the Summary as an opportunity to re-verify the source Fact Find's fields against the Audio to Text file and the recording — not just take the Fact Find's existing values at face value. If a re-check finds an error in the source Fact Find, fix the Fact Find itself (not just the Summary) and say so plainly to the advisor.
- **This rule exists because of a real, caught mistake.** A completed Fact Find marked a home loan's term, monthly repayment and outstanding balance, and a personal loan's outstanding balance, as "Not discussed", when all four were clearly discussed and answered on the call. The advisor caught it after delivery. Treat every "Not discussed" in a transcript-built Fact Find as something to double-check before it goes out, not something to trust because a first pass produced it.
- **Applies to all three mandatory outputs, not just this one.** The Fact Find document, this Summary, and the Planner/AdviceTech Notes recap must all reflect the same, actually-verified facts — never three different guesses at the same figure.

## Mandatory: all totals reconcile to the cent — inclusion is never a judgment call

A Fact Find Summary contains the same underlying figures in more than one place — most importantly, every debt's monthly repayment appears individually in Section 4 (Debt) and then again, combined into a single deduction, in Section 8 (Action Plan — Cover Calculations). These must always agree to the cent. This is a hard requirement, not a best-effort guideline, for the same reason as transcript accuracy above: a silent mismatch between two totals in one document is a compliance problem, not a formatting slip.

- **Compute every total programmatically from the same named components, everywhere it appears.** Never hand-type a total, and never copy a total out of the source Fact Find's own fields (e.g. its own auto-calculated Action Plan / Risk Calculation total) without first re-deriving it from the same component figures used elsewhere in the Summary. If the build script is in Python/reportlab, that means named variables for every component (each debt's monthly repayment, childcare cost, income, etc.), the totals computed by adding them, and an `assert` on each total against its expected value before the PDF is rendered — so a total can never silently drift from the figures that make it up.
- **Debt is never excluded from an income-needed/FNA calculation because it carries credit life — that is not Claude's judgment call to make.** Whether a debt has its own credit life cover is recorded as a fact against that debt (e.g. "Credit life: Yes/No" in Section 4) and nothing more. It must never be used as a reason to leave that debt's monthly repayment out of Section 8's "Total Household Income Needed" deduction, or out of any other cover-needs calculation this skill produces. The client is always quoted cover to include their full debt picture — every debt, every month, regardless of credit life status — unless Paul explicitly instructs otherwise for a specific client.
- **Before delivering, explicitly cross-check every figure that appears in more than one section** (most commonly: Section 4's debt totals vs. Section 8's debt deduction, and any Section 4/Section 8 pair on other product lines that share a component). If two appearances of what should be the same underlying figure disagree, that is the mistake to fix — never explain it away with a footnote or a caveat asking the advisor to confirm which one is "intended". If the disagreement traces back to a stale or incomplete field in the source Fact Find itself (as in the precedent below), fix the source Fact Find too, per the transcript-accuracy section above, and say so plainly to the advisor.
- **Named precedent.** A Fact Find Summary's Section 4 correctly totaled all four debts, because the transcript-accuracy fix had already corrected the home loan's blank fields. But Section 8's "Total Household Income Needed" still deducted only the Fact Find's own stale Action Plan total, computed before that fix and never re-derived from the corrected Section 4 figures. A first attempt to fix this excluded the home loan from Section 8 on the reasoning that its own credit life cover made it unnecessary there. Paul explicitly rejected that: it is a judgment call about insurance methodology that is his to make, not Claude's, and he does not want it made at all. Every debt is always included in full, credit life or not. The corrected version deducts the full total of all debts in Section 8, matching Section 4 exactly, with credit life left as a Section 4 fact only. (Full figures are in `claude/fact-find-summary-changelog.md` where recorded.)
- **Applies wherever the same figure could appear twice** — not just the debt/income-needed pair. Childcare totals, gross/net/take-home income, and any other value used both as a line item and as a component of a further calculation elsewhere in the document follow the same rule. Where the Audio to Text file carries its own totals, recompute them from their components and flag any difference.

## Standing default: Dread Disease Cover Target = R500,000 lump sum

Per Paul's explicit standing instruction (15 September 2026): the **Dread Disease Cover Target** in Section 8 (Action Plan — Cover Calculations) is **always R500,000 lump sum**, unless the transcript or Paul explicitly states a different figure for that specific client. Populate this field with R500,000 as the default whenever dread disease cover wasn't discussed with a different number — it is a fixed standing default, not a blank to leave, guess at, or omit. Only deviate when there's a genuine, sourced reason to (the client requested a different target, or Paul specifies one directly for that client), and when it does deviate, note briefly why (e.g. "R[amount] — client requested a higher target given family history, per the call"). This mirrors the transcript-accuracy and totals-reconciliation standards above: a default is still a claim that must be applied consistently, and any departure from it should be visible and reasoned, not silent.

## Standing ABIs in Section 8 (Paul, 15 October 2026)

In Section 8 (Action Plan — Cover Calculations) these ABI (annual benefit increase) values are fixed for every client, without asking:

- **Life Cover - Fractured Cover:** Life Cover (WOL) ABI = **3%**; Childcare ABI = **5%**.
- **Disability Cover - Fractured Cover:** there is **no** Life Cover (WOL) ABI line (there is no such thing as a Life Cover (WOL) ABI under Disability — never show it). It is replaced by **Household needs ABI = 5%**; Childcare ABI = **5%** stays.
- **Dread Disease ABI = 5%.**

A different figure in the Audio to Text file or the Manual Fact Find is a default, not an answer (named precedent: [client H], 27.10.26 — the Audio to Text file showed a Dread Disease ABI of 3% and had no Household needs ABI line under Permanent disability fractured; 5% was written, the Household needs ABI line was added, and both were mentioned to Paul in one line). Only a different ABI that Paul states explicitly for that client overrides these, with the deviation and its source noted. This does not set the ABI for Standard Life Cover, Standard Permanent Disability or Income Protection, nor any ACI field: those stay as sourced (the Audio to Text file's value if it has one), otherwise `Pending – FNA`. The Summary and the Fact Find (`ff-population-transcript`) must show the same ABIs.

## Quote requests: insurer and New Business vs Servicing (Paul, 15 October 2026)

Any FNA, Shortfall or Reduced quote line in the Summary (Section 7 / Section 9) must name the **insurer** and say whether it is **New Business** or **Servicing**, exactly as in the Fact Find. There is no default: never assume Servicing because the client already holds a policy with that insurer, and never assume the insurer. Take both from the Fact Find (which took them from the Audio to Text file's quote fields and the recording). If the recording and the Fact Find do not say, ask Paul which insurer the FNA, Shortfall or Reduced quote(s) should be requested from (and New Business or Servicing) before the Summary is finalised. If the Audio to Text file states them but the recording is unclear on the insurer, use the Audio to Text value and keep the flag.

## Two spouses in one meeting (Paul, 15 October 2026)

When the Fact Find covers one spouse of a couple who were seen in the same meeting, build one Summary per spouse, each from that spouse's own Fact Find, each saved into that spouse's own `Fact Find` folder (real folder spelling; mention any difference from the name spelling to Paul), each with its own Planner note page and Teams message (`ff-population-transcript` Step 1d has the full rules).

- **Never mix the spouses' information.** Only the debts and the children / childcare are common to both and appear in both Summaries (every debt and every child, in full); a joint policy appears in both, labelled joint. Everything else belongs only to the spouse it was stated about.
- **Opening line:** each Summary starts with one line saying it is a joint meeting with a spouse, covers this spouse only, and that debts and children are common to both.
- **Section 8:** each spouse's own take-home income, minus the full childcare total, minus the full debt repayments. A negative result is shown as calculated, not adjusted, and flagged to Paul.
- **Cross-check before delivering:** search each finished Summary for the other spouse's income, policy numbers and premiums; the only overlap allowed is the common debts, the children and the joint policies.

## Workflow

1. **Locate the source, per the Client File Access rule above and the source order in rule 10.** Do **not** depend on a Fact Find already uploaded to this project, or attached in chat, as the source of truth. Every time this skill is triggered for a named client, check that client's own folder — local machine first (both `Company - Paul` and `Paul Kafentzis (Client Files)`), SharePoint only if unreachable — before touching project files or uploads. List the whole `2. Fact Find` folder and open every candidate (file names vary):
   - If the client's `Fact Find` folder contains a **completed WR Fact Find** document (the `Fact Find Transcript - DD.MM.YY - (Name)` PDF built by `ff-population-transcript`, or another completed Fact Find as PDF or docx), use it directly as this skill's source for the rest of the workflow below. Also open the client's `Fact Find (Audio to Text)` PDF and JSON and the Teams transcript, which the accuracy check in step 3 needs, and the latest Overview in `1. Overview` as a reference.
   - If it instead contains only the Audio to Text file and/or a **Teams meeting transcript** — named `Teams meeting transcript - DD.MM.YY - (Client Name) - FF` or `... _ Annual Review [year]` — with no completed Fact Find yet, this skill does not run standalone: hand off to `ff-population-transcript` first to build and get the advisor's sign-off on the completed Fact Find PDF, then continue this skill's own workflow against that output, chaining into the "one of three outputs" delivery above.
   - Only if a client's folder genuinely has neither a completed Fact Find, an Audio to Text file nor a transcript, and both the local machine and SharePoint have been tried, fall back to checking project files (`/mnt/project/`) and uploads (`/mnt/user-data/uploads/`) — and only ask the user to upload something as the true last resort. Project files persist across conversations, so check there before prompting Paul to re-upload; a quick `ls -la` on both the project directory and the uploads directory at the start of a file-dependent task avoids unnecessary back-and-forth.
2. **Extract all fields.** Read every page of the Fact Find. In the current v3.0 layout the sections are: Client Info; Occupation (incl. duties split); Income & Household; Existing Cover & Retirement; Investments; Medical, Will & Short-Term; Debts; Children; Health; Advisor Actions (Personal risk quotes, Retirement & investment quotes, Referrals & documents, Documents to request); Risk Calculation (Life cover fractured and standard, Permanent disability fractured and standard, Dread disease, Income protection / sickness benefit); and Next Steps & Next Meeting. (A Fact Find built in the older v2.0 layout — Client Info, Occupation, Occupational Duties, Current — Risk/Retirement, Current — Investments/Savings, Medical/Legal/Short-Term, Client Needs, Health, Debts, Children & Childcare, Risk Calculation/Action Plan pages — is read the same way, page by page.) Use the pdf-reading skill if the content isn't already visible in context.
   - **Check the file type before assuming it's a normal PDF.** A completed WR Fact Find sometimes arrives as a `.pdf`-named file that is actually a **zip archive of JPEG page images** rather than a real PDF. If direct PDF text/image extraction fails or looks empty/wrong, inspect the file type first (don't assume the extension is accurate), extract the images if it turns out to be a zip archive, and view the pages sequentially as images. If a supplementary OCR `.txt` file is present alongside it, use it for additional text-layer data rather than re-OCRing yourself.
3. **Verify before trusting.** Cross-check every Fact Find field against the Audio to Text file (PDF and JSON) and the transcript per "Mandatory: transcript accuracy" above — don't just transcribe what the Fact Find document already says. ## Report Structure (fixed section order)

The Summary keeps this fixed nine-section structure whichever layout the source Fact Find is in (Paul, 28/10/2026: keep the old Summary structure; only the sourcing changed). **The Summary carries no source box or source report of its own** — that note goes in the hand-over message (Workflow step 7); the red "Items Requiring Follow-Up" section carries the important flags.

**Where each Summary section comes from in the v3.0 Fact Find:** Client Profile ← Client Info; Occupation & Income ← Occupation + Income & Household; Current Financial Arrangements ← Existing Cover & Retirement, Investments, and Medical, Will & Short-Term; Debt ← Debts; Children & Childcare ← Children; Health ← Health; What the Client Requires ← Advisor Actions (Personal risk quotes, Retirement & investment quotes, Referrals & documents, Documents to request) and the notes of the other sections; Action Plan — Cover Calculations ← Risk Calculation; Items Requiring Follow-Up ← the ⚠ flags, open items and Next Steps & Next Meeting.

1. **Header** — Title "Client Fact Find Summary", client name, advisor, date completed. For a spouse of a couple seen together, add the one-line joint-meeting note described in "Two spouses in one meeting".
2. **1. Client Profile** — name, DOB, ID, cell, work number, personal email, work email, nationality, smoker status, referral from, meeting type, education, marital status/notes.
3. **2. Occupation & Income** — employer, occupation, business interests, occupational duties %, duties description, gross/direct net/take-home income, monthly budget, disposable income.
4. **3. Current Financial Arrangements** — subsections: Risk Cover & Retirement; Investments & Savings; Medical, Legal & Short-Term Insurance.
5. **4. Debt** — one row block per debt with provider, owner/payer, term, monthly, liability, asset, description, credit life, surety; totals at the end.
6. **5. Children & Childcare** — one row per child: name, DOB (and/or age), gender, education stage, monthly cost; total childcare cost at the end. Always include this section if the client has any children on the Fact Find, even if childcare costs are nominal/policy-inclusion figures rather than real spend — note that distinction if the Fact Find's own notes flag it (e.g. figures used for cover-inclusion purposes rather than actual costs).
7. **6. Health** — general health, diabetes, cholesterol, blood pressure, BMI, other health issues/notes.
8. **7. What the Client Requires** — subsections: Personal Risk Cover & Retirement; Investment & Insurance Needs (education planning, general investing, short-term insurance, medical aid/gap cover, will). Include Business Assurance subsection only if Business Owner = Yes on the Occupation page.
9. **8. Action Plan — Cover Calculations** — the section title keeps the words "Action Plan", but its sub-headings are fixed (Paul, 15/10/2026) and must read exactly: **"Life Cover - Fractured Cover"** (then Standard Life Cover), **"Disability Cover - Fractured Cover"** (then Standard Permanent Disability), **"Dread Disease Cover"** and **"Income Protection / Sickness Benefit Cover"** — the words "Action Plan" are never used in those sub-headings. Contents: fractured cover math, ABI percentages (fixed per "Standing ABIs in Section 8" above: Life Cover (WOL) ABI 3% and Childcare ABI 5% under Life Cover - Fractured Cover; Household needs ABI 5% and Childcare ABI 5% under Disability Cover - Fractured Cover, with no Life Cover (WOL) ABI there; Dread Disease ABI 5%), dread disease target (defaults to R500,000 lump sum unless otherwise indicated — see "Standing default: Dread Disease Cover Target" above), income protection terms, life/disability sums assured.
10. **9. Items Requiring Follow-Up** — bulleted list, red text, per step 6 above (name the internal owner per "Internal follow-up routing" above where one applies).

## Styling conventions

- Use reportlab `SimpleDocTemplate`, letter page size, ~18-20mm margins.
- Navy (`#1F3A5F`) for titles/headings, light blue (`#EAF1F8`) shaded label column in two-column tables, grey subtitle line under the title.
- Two-column `Table` per section: left column bold field label (shaded), right column value, 0.5pt grey grid lines.
- Follow-up flags in red text (`#B0271A`), bullet points, no table.
- Filename pattern: `Fact Find Summary - DD.MM.YY - (FirstName Surname).pdf` — use the client's first name followed by surname, space-separated, inside the parentheses (matching the name order used in the Fact Find's "Client Name & Surname" field), and append the Fact Find's "Date Completed" field formatted as DD.MM.YY (e.g. `Fact Find Summary - 27.07.26 - (First Last).pdf`). This is the same `<Title> - DD.MM.YY - (FirstName Surname)` shape used by `ff-population-transcript`'s Fact Find Transcript and `client-teams-transcript-skill`'s Follow-Up Appointment Summary Notes — keep all three consistent.
