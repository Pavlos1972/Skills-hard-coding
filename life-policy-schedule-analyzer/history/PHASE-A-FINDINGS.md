## Facts
- Live skill: ONE file, `SKILL.md`, 874 lines (57,489 bytes). **No script, no template, no asset, no reference page.** The baseline is 802 single rules (sentences) in `SKILL.2026-10-14.md`.
- Job: critically analyse ONE client's life policy schedule (in force) or quote from any SA insurer, on the client's behalf and for Paul's own reference: extract every benefit, verify against the insurer's real product information, benchmark against the SAME insurer's better current options, deep-dive dread disease, tabulate the premium escalation (10 years), cover Vitality/wellness mechanics, payback, cession and second life, list red flags in two tiers (Action Required, For Your Awareness) and give Paul a Broker's Honest Opinion.

## Flaws found

1. **The Broker's Honest Opinion has two voices.** Step 3a says it is "written for Paul as a fellow professional ... Claude's actual read"; Step 5 says "written in first person as Paul's own internal note to himself".
2. **It asks before it looks (S3).** Step 0 asks Paul "Provide details of the Proposed Life Cover Quote" with three options before any search, though the quote and its payback document are normally in the client's folder (`Comparative Quotes`). The board's Life Cover playbook already asks Paul which comparison approach to take: two questions for one decision.
3. **"No product facts are hardcoded" is contradicted.** Scope says insurer-agnostic with no product facts; Steps 0, 2d, 2f and 2g then hardcode BrightRock Trauma IQ, "BR CMPO", Discovery Vitality tiers (Blue to Diamond), PayBack and Change Moment. They can go stale.
4. **The access order is not the house rule (S4).** Local machine through "the device bridge" first, SharePoint only if the machine "genuinely can't be reached", then "ask Paul to attach". Zapier is not tried, nothing is logged.
5. **Out-of-date names and tools.** The Fact Find Summary is looked for as `[FirstName]_[Surname]_Fact_Find_Summary - DD.MM.YY.pdf` (the rebuilt skill saves `Fact Find Summary - DD.MM.YY - (First Surname).pdf` in `2. Fact Find`); `present_files, SendUserFile or this session's equivalent`; `AskUserQuestion`; "the device bridge"; rule 1 "re-load this skill" and rule 9 "propose_skills"; a hand-typed stamp and changelog (S2); the older changelog sits in a Project file (`life-policy-schedule-analyzer-changelog.md`) that is not in the repo or the zip.
6. **The PDF is drawn by whatever script the chat writes each run.** Step 5 says "Use reportlab (SimpleDocTemplate, letter page size)" and gives a palette, but no template or script: the layout can drift between runs. The page size is Letter (South African documents are A4; the Astute skill is A4).
7. **The slide decks depend on a platform tool.** Step 7 builds each report "as a live design using the Artifact tool's Design type" and then "renders a PDF copy ... check the PDF pages visually" with no method for the render. Whether that tool exists in the chats where the skill runs is not stated, and no program can create it.
8. **A premium projection with no stated basis.** Step 2e: where the future rate is not confirmable "project using the best available stated basis and label it illustrative". Nothing says what that basis is, so a number can be invented by the back door; it conflicts with "never a made-up growth rate" in the same bullet.
9. **A rule that pushes against another.** The Goal says a step that "only produces a neutral restatement" has not done its job and to "push further" for a better option; Steps 2c and 3 say never manufacture a finding. They are reconciled only by one sentence in Step 3.
10. **Save rule differs from the house rule.** Step 6.5: never overwrite without checking; if a file exists "keep both by adding a version suffix and tell Paul". The house rule: never overwrite without Paul's yes.
11. **Dates and money (S13):** the old text never says DD/MM/YYYY or how Rand amounts are written; "Athens time" appears only in the stamp.
Nothing forces the rules (text only):
12. **Every client figure is typed by the chat** from the schedule; nothing proves it is in the document, that a total adds up, or that the Benefit Summary Table agrees with the Benefits Breakdown and the Verdict.
13. **"Cross-check against at least a second source" (Step 4)** for the benchmark, dread disease, Vitality and payback sections cannot be shown: no record of which pages were read, when, or what they said.
14. **The top-of-document findings must "also appear, same number, in the full Red Flags section"**, every item is a tier, the filenames follow a pattern, the INTERNAL label must be present and the client decks must not carry it or the Honest Opinion: all are rules with no check.
15. **The "present the Report 2 payback in the table AND on its own slide with the insurer's own figures"** rule has no way to prove the figures came from the payback document.
16. **The save is "verified after saving" by listing the folder**, with no record of the listing; **no access log, run state, reading lock, step driver, checker, tests, package** (S4, S8, S12).

## What a program CAN and CANNOT do here (honest)
- CAN: search the client's folder first and log every route (computer, Microsoft 365, Zapier); show what it found (schedule, proposed quote, the insurer's payback document, Fact Find Summary) on ONE confirmation table; serve each document page by page; check every client figure (sum assured, premium, dates, policy number, waiting and survival periods, beneficiaries) against a verbatim line of the schedule; add up and cross-check totals and that the Benefit Summary Table, the Breakdown and the Verdict agree; compute the 10-year premium table from the stated rate (or copy the insurer's own printed table) and refuse an invented one; require two saved sources with a verbatim line for every product-knowledge statement in the sections that need them; draw the internal PDF in the 17 fixed sections from one data file (A4, the fixed palette, the INTERNAL label, numbered tiered findings that match top and bottom) and the client slide decks from the SAME data (same-insurer policies combined, the four categories always with "Not held", Report 2 only when a proposed quote exists, the payback row and slide with the payback document's own figures, estimates labelled, no Honest Opinion and no INTERNAL or jargon words); read the finished PDFs back and check text and page bounds; derive file names; never overwrite without Paul's yes; list the folder back; print the short audit.
- CANNOT: do the critique itself: whether the product is good, whether a "better current option" really exists and is materially better, the Honest Opinion, whether a finding belongs in Action Required or For Your Awareness; do the web research or prove a saved page is the real, current page; create the live Artifact design (a platform tool); read a scanned schedule (images are marked, shown and attested); prove plain-language wording. **I have never seen a real schedule, quote or payback document from Discovery, BrightRock, Old Mutual, Momentum or Liberty**, so the first real run may need one fix.
- Size: about as big as the skill just finished, because it needs two PDF builders (a portrait internal report and a 1280x720 slide deck) on top of the checking. One pass in this chat, no helpers.
