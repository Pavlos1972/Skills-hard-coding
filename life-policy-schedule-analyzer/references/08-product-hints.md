# Hints from earlier analyses (possible, NOT facts: check each against this client's own documents and the saved sources every time)

The old skill carried these as part of its rules. Insurers change their products, so they are hints about what to look for, never statements to copy into a report.
- **BrightRock:** its needs-matched trauma benefit has been called Trauma IQ: check whether the client and the children are covered under it. Its payback has been the Change Moment Pay-Out, described in a separate guide ("BR CMPO") filed beside the Cover Proposal under the same quote number: take the payback figures only from that document.
- **Discovery:** a wellness-linked pricing mechanism (Vitality, with Blue, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond status) that affects the yearly increase and depends on annual health assessments and, where it applies, medical aid claims; an upfront discount may carry clawback conditions over several years. Its payback has been called PayBack.
- **ACI and ABI** (Annual Contribution Increase and Annual Benefit Increase, or the insurer's own naming) are the components of a stated formula in some products: state each separately and what drives it.
- **A cession** to a bank or lender (for example against a home loan) gives the lender the first right to the claim proceeds up to the amount owed.
