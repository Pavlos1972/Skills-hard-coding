# Step 7: The two documents (drawn by the program)

**The internal report** (A4, one per policy, for Paul only). A polished professional internal document, not client-branded: navy section headings with a thin gold rule, a shaded label column, 0.5pt grey grid lines, every section a table where the content is naturally field and value. The label "INTERNAL - NOT FOR CLIENT DISTRIBUTION" is on every page. Fixed order: Header (report title, client, insurer and product, policy or quote number, date of analysis, the document and where it was found) then the **Verdict** (a boxed callout: one or two sentences, what it is and the single most important thing) then the **Benefit Summary Table** (Benefit / Sum Assured / Premium, one row per benefit held, including each second life's own; common benefit types not held in one compact footer caption, never their own row) then **Action Required / For Your Awareness** (numbered headlines, red and amber) then sections 1 Policy Identity (owner and payer folded into the life assured when identical; life cover type; cession), 2 Benefits Breakdown (a table per benefit with its bullets; the dread disease deep-dive as its own sub-section), 3 Second Life / Joint Life, 4 Premiums (with the 10-year table), 5 Vitality / Wellness Mechanics, 6 Payback / Loyalty Benefit, 7 Benchmark, 8 Exclusions & Special Terms, 9 Riders & Add-Ons, 10 Beneficiaries, 11 Red Flags & Observations (full detail, same numbers), then the **Broker's Honest Opinion** (a light navy-tinted box) then **Sources** (the document and the folder it was found in; the Fact Find Summary used or noted absent; every product source with its address and date; one line that this is an internal reference document, not verified against the insurer's live system, to be reconfirmed before it is relied on for a claim or a compliance purpose). Every statement carries a small tag: "From the document", "Verified: 2 sources", "SINGLE SOURCE", "Inference - to be confirmed" or "Not on the document". Keep it simple: no sub-sub-sections, no nested tables.

**The client slides** (1280x720 pages, one PDF per deck, short, simplified, colourful, for screen-sharing in Teams). Report 1 has six slides, Report 2 seven (see the analysis page). Text that does not fit stops the build; the program never clips silently.

**Checked by the program after the build, by reading the PDFs back:** A4 and 1280x720 page sizes; the INTERNAL label on every report page and on no slide; the sections in the fixed order; the Verdict text; every benefit and sum assured; every numbered finding in both places with the same number; the ten-row premium table; the four categories and "Not held" on the slides; the payback row and the "Paybacks compared" slide in Report 2 with the figures; estimates labelled; no internal wording on a slide; all text inside the page edges. Attested: how it looks, whether the plain English is plain.
