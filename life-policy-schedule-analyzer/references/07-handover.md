# Step 9: What a finished run looks like

A run is finished only when ALL of these are done and the final checker prints no red cross: (1) the source documents were found by the three routes in order, registered and read page by page; (2) every insurer has its saved product sources; (3) every client figure is in the evidence line it cites and every product statement has its saved source line; (4) the mandatory sections were all done (cession, second life, dread disease, Vitality, payback, a benchmark row for every major benefit held, the findings in two tiers, the Verdict and the Honest Opinion); (5) Paul was shown the table and said yes; (6) one internal PDF per policy and the client slide deck(s) were drawn by the program and read back; (7) every PDF was presented to Paul and saved in `Analysis & Comparison Reports`, listed back, nothing overwritten without his yes.

After ANY correction repeat the steps for every deliverable that changed (the driver re-opens them).

**The reply to Paul** has two parts, as they are: first the **tabulated chat summary** (for each policy the Verdict line, the Benefit Summary Table and the numbered Action Required list, a one-line pointer to the Honest Opinion in the PDF, one or two lines per slide report saying what it is and its file name, and the folder the files were saved in) so he gets the gist, including anything he needs to worry about or reconsider, without opening the PDF; then the **SHORT AUDIT** exactly as the program prints it: one line per check with a green tick or a red cross and ONE closing line "Only attested (not proven): ...". Also tell him which assumptions were used (estimates, an assumption of his), the proposed quote's expiry, and any mismatch between the quote and the Fact Find. Any red cross means the job is not finished: say so. Never say the job or the skill is "live" or "done" without the checker's PASS.
