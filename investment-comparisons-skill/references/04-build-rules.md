# What the program builds, and the rules that never change

The program fills the fixed template from your confirmed answers; you never edit the HTML. The layout is fully table-based and Outlook-compatible (inline colours, spacer rows, no flexbox, grid or divs). The colours are the house palette of `life-insurer-comparison`, copied in: a house gold accent (never given to a provider), charcoal for header rows and one fixed colour per provider by position, up to six providers.

**The structure (Step 7): four short tables in this fixed order, no extra narrative sections between them.**
1. **[Product] Comparison** (Retirement Annuity Comparison, Tax-Free Savings Account Comparison, ...): the summary table, one row per feature: plan, contribution, yearly increase, term or retirement age, investment funds, and the once-off costs paid when the plan starts (initial fees and commission). Short, no EAC row. The client's first orientation.
2. **Total Ongoing Fees - Per Annum (Excluding EAC)**: every fee as its own line with a plain explanation, the total added up by the program. This is the visual and analytical anchor of the document: it carries the real cost story and is never shortened or buried. Performance fees appear as a note under it, not in the total.
3. **Investment Fund Comparison**: one row per fund (not per provider): fund, benchmark, target or objective, 1, 3 and 5 year performance, a one-line description, and **each fund's own fact sheet date** in its row (funds from different providers have different dates). All performance data comes from the fact sheets saved for this comparison.
4. **Loyalty and Value-Add Benefits**: loyalty benefit and additional rewards only, kept small (two or three rows per provider, not a paragraph per provider). A plain explanation block comes before the table when a mechanism needs one.

Every table has a short plain-English intro line under its heading saying what it shows and why it matters (fixed wording in `assets/wording.json`). A short **"Things to know"** box above the first table appears ONLY when something must be flagged: quotes that do not match, a Tax-Free quote that would breach a limit, a supplementary document that disagrees with its quote, a stale fact sheet (more than 92 days old) or a missing one. The optional parts (tax note, flexibility, fees-only comparison) come after Table 4, only if Paul asked.

**The email around the tables.** It opens "Dear [first name], Following our Teams Meeting on [the meetings already held, weekdays computed], herewith please find a comparison of the [product] quotes from [the providers]:" and closes "Hoping that above is in order." and the bold next-meeting line, like Paul's proposal emails. It carries the standard header (From Paul, To the client, Cc Sitamah Nathoo, Subject built by the program, Importance High), his signature block and the logo (full copy only). There is no separate covering email: the opening and closing lines are the cover.

**What the program checks on the built email (Step 8 of the old skill, now proven):** no placeholder; tables open = close; the header; the weekdays; the four tables in the fixed order with an intro line under each heading; the fee totals equal the figures shown; no EAC headline (the word appears only in the fee table's title and its explanation); every industry term (EAC, TER, TIC, commission, ...) is explained where it is used; every provider, fund and fund-sheet date is there; the "Things to know" box exists only when something is flagged; the optional parts exist only when asked; the logo is only in the full copy. **Only attested:** that the plain English is really plain (read it as if seeing it for the first time with no financial background), that a loyalty explanation is clear enough, and how it looks.

**Files:** the full copy (with the logo) is sent to Paul in the chat as `[FirstName]_[Surname]_Investment_Comparison_Email - DD.MM.YY.html`; the no-logo copy is saved in the client's Comparative Quotes; `draft_body.html` is pasted into the Outlook draft (from "Dear X," to the bold next-meeting line, with no header, no "Kind regards," and no signature). The subject is "[Client full name] / [Product] Comparison - [Provider A] vs [Provider B]".

**The save.** Save exactly the file the program names. Never truncate or retype the logo (base64) by hand. Use `sharepoint_upload_file` (driveId + parentItemId, `expectedBytes` = the file size, `conflictBehavior: "replace"` only after Paul's yes). Tell Paul the folder and the file name in plain words, and that the saved copy has no logo.
