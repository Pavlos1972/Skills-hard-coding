## What the skill does now, in plain words
- A program walks the chat through 10 steps and refuses to skip one: find the quotes and the Fact Find (computer, then Microsoft 365, then Zapier, every try logged), name the funds and fetch each fund's current fact sheet (the web, then Chrome, every try logged), read everything page by page, enter the answers, let the program work out the comparison, show you ONE confirmation table and ask ONE question, build, save, draft, message Sitamah, final audit.
- **Every figure is checked against the right document's text:** the premium, yearly increase, term, funds, allocations, every fee, the once-off costs and the loyalty percentages (the quote); every benchmark, target, 1/3/5-year figure and fact sheet date (the saved fact sheet); the client's name, email and risk profile (the Fact Find). A fee line that is a blended or total figure is refused (so is a quote that shows only EAC). The allocations must add up to 100%. A quote must say it is the product being compared.
- **The program does the sums and the comparisons:** the blended asset management fee, the total ongoing fees (added up from the figures as shown), apples-to-apples across the quotes (contribution structure, amount, yearly increase, term), the Tax-Free room (once you confirm the limits), stale and missing fact sheets, and a flag when a fund's risk label differs from the client's profile (flag for you only, never shown to the client).
- **The email is built from a fixed template:** the four tables in the fixed order, each with a plain-English intro line; every fee row explained in plain words; every fund shows its OWN fact sheet date; a short "Things to know" box only when something must be flagged (quotes that do not match, a Tax-Free limit breach, a supplementary document that disagrees with its quote, a stale or missing fact sheet); the tax note, flexibility and a fees-only comparison only if you ask. The program checks that no EAC figure appears as a headline (the word is only in the fee table's title and its explanation) and that every industry term is explained where it is used.
- **Nothing is sent without your yes.** The full copy goes to you in the chat; a no-logo copy is saved in the client's Comparative Quotes (never overwriting without your yes); the Outlook draft (To the client, Cc Sitamah, never sent by Claude) is read back and checked; then the Teams message to Sitamah goes through Zapier. If the quotes do not match, the one question asks you whether to go on, and the email labels the mismatch.
- The reply to you is the short audit: one line per check with a tick or a cross.
