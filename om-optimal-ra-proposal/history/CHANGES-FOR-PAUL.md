## What the skill does now, in plain words
- A program walks the chat through 10 steps and refuses to skip one: find the documents (computer, then Microsoft 365, then Zapier, every try logged), read them page by page, enter the answers, **run the Tax Reform Tool**, show you ONE confirmation table and ask ONE question, build, save, draft, message Sitamah, final audit.
- **The program runs the Tax Reform Tool itself.** It writes the client's figures into a copy of the workbook, lets LibreOffice recalculate it four times (R 0 RA, today's RAs, the maximum RA, the proposed RA), reads the answers from the named cells, and then works every figure out a second time on its own from the 2026/27 tax tables. If the two ever disagree, or LibreOffice is missing, the run stops. Claude never types a tax figure again, and the three tables and the bar chart can no longer be mixed up.
- **Every figure from the documents must be in the right document's text:** the premium, start date, funds, allocations, every fee and the monthly admin fee (the quote); name, age, employer, position, income and email (the Fact Find Summary); each existing RA's insurer, policy number, contribution, value and date (the Astute report or the Client Overview); the fund statement's rand amounts, percentages, value and date; the RGAR's five figures. Where the label can be found, the figure must sit next to it. The fund allocations must add up to 100%.
- **The program does the sums you used to do by hand:** the blended asset management fee and the total ongoing fees, every weekday, the three tables' rows (one named row per existing RA in Table 1, one new RA row in Table 2, replace or keep in Table 3, the fund rows in all three), the "what you can save" sentence for the client's situation, the orange message under each table, and the bar chart.
- **The old 12-point audit is checked by the program** (checks 1 to 3 and 6 to 9 must hold; checks 4 and 5 are worked out and, when the two figures differ, **flagged to you with both figures** instead of forced, your known edge case). In my own test with a pension client they differed, as you said they can: you will see the flag in the table and in the audit.
- The email comes from the fixed template (no placeholder can remain). The full copy has the logo, the saved no-logo copy goes in the RA transaction folder's Comparative Quotes (never overwriting without your yes), the chat makes the Outlook draft and gives the program the read-back, and only then does the Teams message go to Sitamah (through Zapier, after your one yes). The reply to you is the short audit.
- **When a lump sum goes in, it is a second run** (`--document lump_sum`) with its own answers, email, draft and message. Section 14 or voluntary is your answer; for Section 14 the amount is the sum of the NET fund values (the program checks each figure sits next to "Net fund value", so a gross figure is refused). A voluntary lump sum stops until you give the tax-treatment note.
- **The tax-year guard:** from 01/03/2027 the program stops, because the workbook and `assets/tax_facts.json` are the 2026/27 tables. To renew them, both files are changed in Claude Code.

## What a program cannot prove (still your eye or your judgement)
- That a figure sits in the right row of its document where its label was not found, and anything on a scanned page (payslips, Astute pages and fund statements are often images): the program says so and the chat must view them.
- That the RGAR figures are the SECOND shortfall table and that the RGAR matches the product being proposed.
- The paste into Outlook and the Zapier send themselves: the program builds the exact text and checks the read-back, but cannot see Outlook or Teams. That the draft looks right in Outlook, that the files really sit in the folder and that the message really arrived.
- The advice itself, and the replace-or-keep decision (your answer, recorded).
- **Not yet proven on a real client.** I have never seen a real quote from Alwyn, a real Astute report, fund statement or RGAR. The checks search for the values next to their labels rather than assuming a layout, but a label I guessed ("Regular contribution", "Net administration fee", "Net fund value", "Years until retirement"...) may be worded differently, in which case the program only proves the figure is somewhere in the document. The first real run may need one fix.
- **LibreOffice must be available in the chat that runs the skill.** The old skill already relied on it. The program checks at the start and stops with a clear message if it is missing.
