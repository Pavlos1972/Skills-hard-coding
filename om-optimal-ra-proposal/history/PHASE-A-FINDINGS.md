3. **Who sends the client email:** this skill says Sitamah attaches the documents and emails the client; `client-pipeline-board` (send-decision-rules and the RA playbook) says the proposal lands in Drafts and Paul sends it himself.
4. **Step 8c sends the Teams message to Sitamah as soon as the draft is verified, with no yes from Paul** (S10). (It already uses Zapier with markdown: correct.)
5. **The MCD contradicts the skill in several places:** it says [CLIENT EMAIL] and [CC EMAIL] are "left as placeholder, the adviser fills in" and the audit excepts them (the skill: To = the client's real email, Cc always Sitamah Nathoo); it describes the old workflow "Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste into Outlook" (the skill: Chrome paste into a draft); its audit has 10 checks (the skill 12); it says "Aptos 11pt throughout" while the skill says "match the surrounding body text" and the template uses Calibri; its footer and Sections 2 and 5.1 still cite the old template file name and "Version 5.0" (the skill's own note admits this and says to flag it to Paul).
6. **The template is stale on three points and Claude must override it by hand every run** (the skill admits it, found 01/10/2026): the static "what you can save" sentence uses [MAXIMUM RA CONTRIBUTION] and one wording (the rule needs [TOTAL CAP MONTHLY] and six scenario variants; the placeholder does not exist in the template), "You will be working" (the rule is "You are working at"), and the row label for the new RA. Nine placeholders in the MCD list are not in the template and twelve in the template are not in the list (for example [MONTHLY REMUNERATION] has no stated source).
7. **"C15" means two different cells.** Step 3 uses "C15" for the binding SARS cap (it is Calcs!C15) and later for the monthly remuneration INPUT (it is Maximise Allowance Tool!C15). I checked the workbook: both are true, on different sheets, and the text names the sheet only once. A wrong guess gives wrong tax figures.
8. **The employment-type switch is unclear:** "select 'Self Employed' by writing to Calcs!B2". B2 holds the text "Tax Monthly" and the formulas compare it with column-A labels that do not read like employment types. Not verified here: it must be tested before a program relies on it.
9. **Step 1 asks Paul 12 questions;** items 1 to 6 and 12 are documents to find in the client's file (S3: file first, Paul last), not questions.
Nothing forces the rules (text only):
10. **The tax tool is run by hand three times** (RA = 0, the maximum, the proposed premium) and the skill itself says "mixing them up is the single easiest mistake". Nothing proves which run each table came from. A program can run all three itself from the answers.
11. **Every figure is typed with no proof:** from the quote (premium, start date, funds, allocations, fees, monthly fixed admin fee), the Fact Find Summary, the Astute/Overview (each existing RA's insurer, policy number, monthly contribution, gross and net fund value), the fund statement and proof of income (pension/provident contributions) and the RGAR (the SECOND shortfall figure, years, current and future value, retirement age).
12. **The blended asset management fee and the total ongoing fees** ("calculate from allocation %") are added up by hand.
13. **The three-table logic and the replace-or-keep rows** (existing RA rows in Table 1, a single new RA row in Tables 2 and 3, both in the keep case; "Provident" never mixed with "Pension"; tax saved never R– when an RA exists) are rules with no check. The bar chart's ten values are copied by hand.
14. **The 12 audit checks are done by eye.** Checks 1 to 9 are arithmetic relations a program can prove from the recalculated outputs; checks 4 and 5 "can legitimately fail" with no rule for when.
15. **No date script in this skill** (the Tax-Free and RGAR skills have one): the meeting weekdays in the opening and closing lines are typed. The RGAR skill records a real email whose weekday was wrong.
16. **No access log** (S4): SharePoint tools only.
Vague or missing:
17. **Voluntary-funds lump sum:** the tax-treatment note ([LS_TAX_TREATMENT_NOTE]) has no approved wording; the MCD says "flag this to the adviser rather than assuming".
18. **Tax-year dependence:** the workbook and the MCD are the 2026/27 tables (brackets, the R17,820 rebate, the R430,000 cap, valid until 28/02/2027). Nothing stops a proposal being built on stale tables after 01/03/2027.
Standards and clutter:
19. About 30% of the 253 lines are the copied universal rules, the Delivery gate, the propose_skills rule (replaced by S7), the standing-rule block and the changelog; the stamp and changelog are typed by hand (S2). The Chrome draft method is a long paragraph. No run state, no tests, no checker.

20. **Table 1's tax-tool input is described two ways:** Step 3 says "Table 1: RA=0 / current situation"; the MCD (V5.0 and Section 8.2) says Table 1 shows the client's current position with every existing RA as a named row and the tax saved on them (never R–). With an existing RA, "RA=0" would be wrong. The rebuild reads it the MCD way: Table 1's RA input = the total of the existing RAs' monthly contributions (0 only when the client has none); Table 3 in the keep case = existing + proposed.

## What a program CAN and CANNOT do here (honest)
- CAN: force the steps; find and serve the documents page by page; check every figure and name against the right document's text; **run the Tax Reform Tool itself for the three scenarios** (LibreOffice recalculation, sheet and cell named each time) and read the three tables and the ten bar values from the outputs; add up the blended fee and the totals; build the tables, rows and sentences from fixed rules (replace/keep, Pension/Provident label, the six "what you can save" sentences); prove audit checks 1 to 3 and 6 to 9 and compute checks 4 and 5, flagging a difference as the known edge case; compute every weekday; fill the fixed templates; check the draft and Teams read-backs; stop after the tax year ends.
- CANNOT: read scanned pages (Astute, fund statements, payslips are often images: the chat views them and it stays attested); recalculate in a chat that has no LibreOffice (the program checks first and stops); judge whether the proposal is suitable; know how a real Astute report or Alwyn's quote is laid out (never seen: the first real run may need one fix); paste into Outlook or send on Zapier.
