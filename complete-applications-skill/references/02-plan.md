You never write the PDF. You write ONE file, `plan.json`; the program checks it against the real form's own field list and rules and fills the form itself.

```
{"mode": "prefill" | "correct",
 "listing": {"applications": ["names of the files already in the Applications folder"]},
 "apps": [ ... ]}
```

**A new application (prefill).** One entry per application: one per product AND per contribution type (a recurring RA and a lump sum Section 14 transfer into the same plan are two). `id`, `form` (`om` for RA, Tax-Free and Flexi; `general` for risk covers with any insurer), `insurer` (`name`; a name other than Old Mutual or Brightrock also needs `abbr` and `abbr_paul_said`), `product` (OM: `option` = one of the form's own products, `type` = Recurring, Lump Sum or Section 14 transfer, and for a product the program does not know `short` and `short_paul_said`; General: `cover_line` = Personal Covers, Business Covers, Buy & Sell, Contingent Liability or Keyman Cover), `kind` (`recurring` or `lump`), `amount` (a number), `meeting_date` (DD/MM/YYYY, the signature date), `fields`, `replacement`, `txn_folder` (`root` New Business or WR Clients, `current_name`, `is_current_transaction: true`, and `candidates` plus `paul_said` when more than one could be current) and, only with Paul's yes, `overwrite.paul_said`.

**fields.** Each field is `{"value": "...", "evidence": {"doc": "<role>", "line": "<a verbatim line of that document>"}}` or `{"value": "...", "paul_said": "<his words>"}`. The value's words or digits must be in its line; a dropdown value must be one of the form's options. The OM form's keys are its field names (see `field-mappings.md`); the General form's keys are listed there too (title, first_names, surname, id_number, cell ...). Leave out anything the file does not hold. Never enter a field the program builds: `Product`, `SignatureDate`, `IsReplacement`, `ReplacedPolicyNumbers` and, where they do not apply, `ForeignTaxNumber`, `OriginateFrom`, `DeferredPremiumFacility`, `MaritalContractType`; for General the insurer, cover line, the replacement fields and the date. The client's signature is never written.

**The rules the program enforces.**
- **Product** is the form's own product plus the amount: `<base product> - R 2,800.00 p.m.` (recurring) or `<base product> - R 100,000.00` (lump sum); the new value is added to the closed dropdown right after its base option. Amounts are always `R` with thousands commas and two decimals.
- **Replacement is a real answer, not a guess.** `replacement: {"answer": "Yes|No", "source": {...}, "policies": [{"insurer", "policy_no", "evidence": {"doc": "policy_schedule", "line": ...}}]}`. Not a replacement: OM `ReplacedPolicyNumbers` = N/A, General existing company and policy number blank. A replacement is written `<Insurer>, Policy No. <number>`, several separated by `; `, and the number is checked against the schedule document, not an older draft. If the file is contradictory or silent, ask Paul rather than guessing.
- **Foreign tax number follows foreign tax obligation** (No gives N/A). **`OriginateFrom`** is filled only when `OwnKnowledge` = No, otherwise N/A. **`DeferredPremiumFacility`** is N/A for a lump sum. Any field with an N/A option that does not apply gets N/A, not blank (a Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated client's marriage contract type is N/A).
- **The signature date** is the meeting date, `DD/MM/YY`, never blank. `DateOfEntry` is `DD/MM/YYYY`. The General form's debit date cannot be the 10th to the 14th.
- **Flags, never refusals:** an ID number whose date part or check digit is wrong, beneficiary shares that do not add up to 100%, an email or phone number that does not look right.

**A correction.** `mode: "correct"`; each entry has `id`, `current_id` (the id you registered with `run.py current`) and `changes`: `{key: {"value", "evidence" | "paul_said"}}` or `{key: {"from_app": {"id": "<another current application>", "key": "<its key>"}}}` (the program copies the value). A detail carries across only to the same person for that role, between the two forms only for the one-to-one pairs in `forms.json` (title, ID number, cell, work phone, email, residential code, account holder, bank, branch code, account type, account number, beneficiary ID, relationship and share), and OM to OM or General to General for anything not product-specific. Product-specific answers (replacement, DeferredPremiumFacility, source of funds, declarations, debit date, doctor, children) are never carried unless Paul says so (`paul_said`). If two current applications hold different values for the same detail, the program STOPS: ask Paul. A garbled or ambiguous value, a different person, or conflicting sources: ask first. A standing carry-across instruction from Paul is followed without asking again for those details. Signature fields are never touched.
