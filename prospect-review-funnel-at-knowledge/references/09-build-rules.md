## AT build rules

- Names in AT match the documents exactly. No "Claude" in any AT name.
- Every item except sub-headings starts with "SR – ", "PK – " or "CL – " (Decision 12).
- Sub-headings, bold outcome rows (A6 outcomes; A10, A10a, A10b outcomes) and AUTOMATION rows are typed in CAPITALS.
- An AUTOMATION row that opens a module ends with "— TICK DONE TO OPEN BELOW" (Decision 11); the hand-over lines that only move the card (D55) say "— IF THE ANSWER BELOW IS YES" or "— WHATEVER THE ANSWER BELOW".
- Lines that apply to some cases only end "— if applicable" in shared modules and are set to Skip, never struck out (Decision 7).
- Family A / B sub-tasks are always created WITH a Sub Category; Families A and B keep no Category checklist.
- Mutually exclusive stages never share a checklist. Each decision outcome has its own AUTOMATION line and trigger.
- Teams invite (Decision 19): each Family A starter #31–#38 gets "SR – Send Teams meeting invite to client with agreed date and time".
- Paul approves every AT change and watches in Chrome; Claude never deletes anything in AT; tests only on Mr John Snow. The auto-mode classifier blocks AT writes until Paul approves that change in chat; once he says do everything at once, the whole batch is covered.
