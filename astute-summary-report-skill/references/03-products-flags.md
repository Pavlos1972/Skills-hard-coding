Contents: checking products online, and the flags.

**Products (one search per distinct product name, not per policy):** search for the insurer's own current product page or guide. Record the web address and a description of at most 30 words that says what the product is (structure and terms only). If you cannot confirm it online, record it as unconfirmed: the table then says "Not confirmed online. Described only as named in the report." and an orange flag is raised automatically. Never invent a description.

**Flags the program raises itself:** a provider that returned no data (not found, unable to match, no row); zero or missing values; a policy with zero values that Astute does not label paid-up (it is NOT shown as paid-up); a status it does not recognise; an unconfirmed product; a total that differs from the report's own total; a policy marked as held with a bank's insurance arm.

**Flags only you can judge** (add each with a reason in words): `status_contradiction` (for example a package marked Active whose underlying policies are Inactive), `unknown_product` (an unknown or thinly described product), `wrapper_ambiguity` (an RA versus endowment wrapper ambiguity), `bank_arm` (a policy held with a bank's insurance arm), `other` (anything else Paul should verify). If none apply, say so with a reason.

The numbered flag list in your reply is most important first: status contradictions, unknown products, wrapper ambiguities, providers with no data, zero or missing values, mismatched totals, unconfirmed products, bank insurance arms, other.
