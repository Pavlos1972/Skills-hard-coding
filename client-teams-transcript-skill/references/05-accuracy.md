## Accuracy (both outputs, always)

- No fact, figure, name or instruction may be dropped or guessed. Headline figure per topic only, but exact. Do not editorialise or add advice that was not discussed.
- **Be resourceful before you ask or flag:** a detail missing from one document is almost always in another - check the quote, then the Fact Find Summary / Fact Find, the latest WR Overview, earlier Follow-Up Notes, the applications and current correspondence before writing "to confirm" or handing a lookup to Sitamah or Paul.
- **Resolve before you flag:** use the Overview, quotes, Fact Find and earlier notes to resolve garbled figures and names. Flag only what is still genuinely ambiguous.
- **ALL ACTION POINTS GO TO SITAMAH.** Every action in Output 1 and Output 2 is assigned to **Sitamah** - what's-next emails, FICA requests, meeting invites, application updates/chasing, emailing any provider, requesting or chasing quotes, sending documents, diarising follow-ups. Even if the transcript has Paul saying "I'll email"/"I'll send", write it as `Sitamah to ...`. Paul is never listed as the owner of an action point. Insurer/provider contacts appear as the party being contacted, not as invented owners. **This rule applies identically at a first/Fact Find meeting.**
- **Two genuinely independent Sitamah actions are never worded as one waiting on the other, unless the transcript or Paul actually said they depend on each other.** A request to an insurer/provider and a separate request to the client for a supporting document are usually independent - write them as two parallel bullets ("Sitamah to request X from [provider] now" / "Sitamah to separately request Y from the client"), not as "request X once Y is received" or "X, pending Y", unless the call or Paul specifically established that dependency. **Named precedent:** [client B], 07.10.26 (review) - a first draft wrote the BrightRock Servicing FNA request as "pending the updated psychiatric report", implying Sitamah must wait for the report before requesting the FNA from BrightRock. Paul corrected this: the FNA is requested from Lizelle Cachado now, with the Fact Find's changes and all the same loadings, independently of the separate request to the client for the report - the two run in parallel.
- **NO INFERENCE.** Record what was SAID, not what seems likely, usual or implied. Never invent a fact about the client (above all health, family, money or legal facts) and never invent an action. Every statement must pass the Step 1b source trace. When the transcript is garbled and two readings are possible, flag it to Paul instead of choosing a reading.
- **Record the client's answer exactly as given.** If the client responded positively, say so (e.g. he said "Alright, 100%" to an offer - do not write "not agreed"); if he declined or put something on hold, say that (not "still open" for Sitamah to chase).
- **Only actions that were actually given.** An action exists only if Paul said it, the client asked for it (e.g. "put it in your email"), or Paul clearly instructed it. Requests the client makes of Paul/Sitamah and offers Paul makes (e.g. offering to review a spouse's portfolio, to be raised in the what's-next email) are captured as `Sitamah to ...` actions. Items left "for now" or where the client will get back to us are NOT chase actions. Anything Paul remarks on in passing that is not an instruction (e.g. a missing item on the Overview) is flagged to Paul as a question, not written as an action - unless Paul then confirms it.
- Rand amounts as **R**, exactly as stated.
