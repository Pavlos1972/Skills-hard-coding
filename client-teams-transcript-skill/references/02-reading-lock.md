## Step 1b: THE TWO-READ LOCK AND STRICT COMPLETENESS PROTOCOL (Paul's absolute rule - non-negotiable, there is NO way around it)

**THE LOCK: this skill is NOT ALLOWED to draft, show or save Output 1 or Output 2 until (a) Pass 1, (b) Pass 2 and (c) the source trace below have ALL been completed.** Not for a long transcript, not for a first meeting, not for a re-run, not for a quick request, not because the call looks simple. If either read cannot be completed, say exactly what could not be read and stop - do not produce a partial report.

These rules exist because a decision made late in a call can silently change what an earlier part of the call said (e.g. a Tax-Free first set up for a child, then the client decides it should be in his own name for now), because garbled speech-to-text can be turned into a false fact, and because requests made in the closing minutes are the easiest to miss. Reporting the earlier state as final, inventing a fact, or missing a request are serious errors.

1. **PASS 1 - read EVERYTHING, in order, from the first line to the last.** The program serves the converted text in numbered chunks (`tx.py chunk`, 400 lines or fewer each) and refuses to skip one; never sample, skim, search-only or stop early. Confirm the last chunk reaches the closing "stopped transcription"/goodbye. Facts can sit anywhere, including filler chatter, a form being completed live, and the closing minutes. **A read done by another skill (e.g. `ff-population-transcript`) does NOT count as either pass** - this skill does both of its own reads.
2. **Keep a decision ledger while reading Pass 1** (`run.py ledger add`, kept in the run folder, never saved to the client's folder): every decision, acceptance, refusal, "let me think", change of mind, correction of a name/figure/date, every new fact (owner, payer, beneficiary, product, amount, start date, address, bank, who does what, next appointment), **every request or instruction either person makes** (what Sitamah must send or ask, what the client must send, what goes in the what's-next email, offers Paul makes such as reviewing a spouse's portfolio, things put on hold), each with its line number, the exact words and the speaker.
3. **PASS 2 - a second, separate, COMPLETE read.** After the Pass 1 ledger exists and BEFORE any output is written, read the ENTIRE transcript again from the first line to the last, in chunks, this time checking every ledger entry against the actual words and hunting for anything Pass 1 missed - especially the closing minutes, the client's questions, anything the client asks Paul/Sitamah to do, anything Paul offers, anything left "for now", and every figure and name. Correct or add ledger entries as needed. **A keyword scan does NOT count as a read, and neither does re-reading only the parts that look important.**
4. **The FINAL state governs.** If something is decided or changed later in the call, the later statement overrides the earlier one. Report the final state as the current position and record the earlier state as `Changed` (before -> after, and who decided). Never present a superseded state as current.
5. **Keyword scan (in addition to, never instead of, the two reads)** - `tx.py scan` searches the transcript for decision and change words (the list below is built into it) - `change`, `instead`, `rather`, `actually`, `for now`, `later`, `keep it`, `leave it`, `get back to you`, `in your name`, `his name`, `her name`, `owner`, `beneficiary`, `payer`, `address`, `spell`, `wrong`, `correct`, `sort that out`, `tomorrow`, `think about`, `go ahead`, `happy`, `not happy`, `agree`, `decline`, `cancel`, `increase`, `reduce`, `put it in`, `email`, `send`, `wife`/`husband`/`partner`, `credit life`, `retrench`, `instalment`/`installment`, `protector`, `cede`/`cession`/`ceded`, `payback`/`payout`, `bank`, `bond`, `card`, `waiting period`, `months`, `pays`/`covers`, `date`, `Monday`...`Friday`, amounts - and make sure every hit is reflected in the ledger and outputs. Every hit in the closing minutes needs a ledger entry within 3 lines or a recorded review.
6. **Resolve garbled words using context** from the Overview, quotes, Fact Find and earlier notes, then flag only what is still ambiguous. **A garbled line is NEVER turned into a fact.** Read the whole exchange around it; if it can mean opposite things (e.g. "I've got no, I've got a medical condition that I know of" can mean he HAS or has NO known condition), do not assert either reading - flag it to Paul as garbled and leave it out of the outputs (or, for a health or other sensitive item, ask Paul before writing anything). Digit-by-digit read-outs are never trusted from the transcript - they are checked against the live application (Step 2b, follow-up only) and never written into the outputs.
7. **SOURCE TRACE before drafting (the third gate):** for EVERY bullet in Output 1/Output 2 and EVERY action point, record the transcript line(s) and the exact words it comes from (`src` in content.json), or the named document and the exact words it comes from (Overview, quote, Fact Find Summary, earlier note, correspondence). **Anything with no source is deleted. Nothing is added because it "would make sense", "is usual", or "seems implied".** In particular: an action is only written if Paul said it, the client asked for it, or Paul clearly instructed it; "leave it for now / the client will get back to us" is NOT a chase action; an item the client declined or put on hold is reported as on hold, not as an open task for Sitamah.
8. **Cross-check the final state against hard evidence:** client replies in Outlook (Step 1c), the live applications (Step 2b, follow-up only), the quotes and the Overview.
9. **Coverage check before delivery:** walk the ledger entry by entry (`run.py ledger list`) and cover each one with `run.py ledger cover`; `verify.py` fails any decision, request, change or hold that no item covers (every request/instruction in the ledger must map to a Sitamah action or a status line). Then do a **verification pass of the drafted outputs against the transcript**: any wrong person, wrong figure, wrong date, wrong status, contradiction, invented item, or action wrongly worded as conditional on another when the two are actually independent (per the rule in Accuracy below). For a meeting that includes an acceptance, a change of owner/beneficiary/payer, or money amounts, use an independent verification (a subagent where the Agent tool is available) to re-check the key facts against the transcript.
10. **Say it in the delivery message (one line):** `verify.py` prints it ("Transcript read in full TWICE (Pass 1 and Pass 2, N lines each)", the number of ledger entries, confirmation that every bullet and action was source-traced); copy it unedited, and report any change-of-mind items as `Changed`. **It is printed only when the checks pass, so if it is not there the report must not be delivered.**
