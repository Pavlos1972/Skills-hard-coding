Contents: the template's fixed rules, the reference number, the file name and the constants. The program applies all of them; this page is what it applies.

- **The template's dropdowns are native Word content controls.** The exact option lists are in `dropdown_field_map.md` (and the program reads them from the template itself). Every choice must be a real option; the program refuses anything else.
- **Need Identified:** Death is always "Life Cover" and Dread always "Comprehensive Severe Illness" (hard-coded by the script); Retirement is always "Retirement Planning"; Investment is "Children Education Planning" or "General Investment Planning"; Lump Sum Disability and Income Protection (1st 24 months) must be given: "Lump Sum Disability Cover" and "Sickness and/or Income Protection Cover".
- **Retirement and Investment:** Recommended Amount and Taken Up Amount rows are removed entirely (never blank or "N/A"). Need Identified, Insurers Considered, Recommended Insurer and Recommendation for Identified Need remain.
- **Insurers Considered:** at least 1 and up to 3 slots, never padded; unused slots stay visually blank. The "." option is a template error: never selected. The fixed list is ALTRISK, CAPITAL LEGACY, DISCOVERY, MOMENTUM, LIBERTY, SANLAM, OLD MUTUAL, BRIGHTROCK, STANLIB. An insurer or product outside the lists (Ninety-One, Allan Gray, Coronation, ...) is written directly into the field in CAPITALS (for example NINETY-ONE) and Paul is told in the confirmation table.
- **Recommendation for Identified Need, Old Mutual retirement annuity:** type the client's ACTUAL product name (for example "Old Mutual Retirement Annuity Xtra Max"), never a hard-coded "Old Mutual Optimal Retirement Annuity" and not "Old Mutual - Max Investment" for this product. This has regressed before: the program shows the product in the confirmation table.
- **Financial Planner:** always PAUL KAFENTZIS. FSP number 45946. **Next review date:** always the ROA date plus 12 months, computed by the script.
- **Out-of-bundle and declined sections:** Status "Do not Review", the data table removed, the reason box reads "This need was not considered in this Review meeting." (declined needs carry their own reason). Income Protector Planning After 24 Months is always Do not Review with "This need has been worked into the client's Lump Sum Disability Cover Benefit." (BrightRock structural rule). A need that exists but the client will not pursue: "The client did not want to consider this need at this time."
- **Policy Replacement wording** is replace when ANY cover type is a replacement, not replace otherwise.
- **The script also:** deletes the Health Care section and the RAR reference number, collapses dual lump-sum/monthly amounts to one value (for example "R 37,000 monthly"), reorders the Retirement merged table (Need Identified, Insurers Considered, Recommended Insurer, Recommendation, Reason), strips data bindings and the placeholder-grey style so filled fields are black, keeps the client's accept/decline block on one page, and trims the trailing blank paragraphs.
- **ROA Reference No. (in the document):** Risk `[Insurer] [Personal|Business] Cover - R[premium] p.m.` (example: `Old Mutual Personal Cover - R1,555.10 p.m.`); Retirement and Investment `[Product Provider] [Product Name] - R [amount][ - Lump Sum | p.m.]` (examples: `Old Mutual Retirement Annuity Xtra Max - R 158,192.00 - Lump Sum` and `... - R 1,500.00 p.m.`).
- **File name (derived by the program, never typed):** Risk `ROA - [Insurer] [Personal|Business] Cover - R [premium] p.m. - [DD.MM.YY] - ([Client Full Name])`; insurer in title case except BrightRock, always "BR"; the premium has no thousands comma, 2 decimals and a space after "R". Retirement and Investment `ROA - [Product Provider] [Product Name] - R [amount][ - Lump Sum | p.m.] - [DD.MM.YY] - ([Client Full Name])`. The date is always the ROA date.
- **Known template quirks:** the advisor's bottom "Signature: ... Date:" line has no control, so the script appends the ROA date; the "Client: SIGNATORIES" date is deliberately left blank for the client's own signature; "Presented By ... Print Date" is a live Word field and will show today's date when opened.
