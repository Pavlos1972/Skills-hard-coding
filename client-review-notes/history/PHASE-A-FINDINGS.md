## Facts
- Live skill: ONE file, `SKILL.md`, 297 lines (20 KB). No script, no template, no other file. **209 rules** counted in the archived baseline (`SKILL.2026-10-14.md`, identical to the live text).
- Job: turn the raw notes of a client's Outlook contact into ONE standard Client Record, delivered as a downloadable `.md` file. A header line (`Firstname (Known) Lastname (Client) (ID)`), then 18 fields in a fixed order (Full Name, Last Name, First Name, Known As, Categories, First meeting, First policy signed, Portfolio Review Month, Got details from, Client ID No, Income Tax Number, Adviser Allocation, Medical Aid, Short Term Insurance, Family Information, Life & Investments with three sub-headings, Notes, Commission). Layout: bold label, value on the next line, `---` between sections, no sign-off. Notes in chronological order under BOLD ALL CAPS date headings, summarised not transcribed ("every material fact must still appear"); structured fields (contract numbers, DOC dates, premiums, status notes, IDs, commission) verbatim, typos kept and flagged with a warning sign; "do not contact" lines verbatim and flagged at the top of Notes; a duplicate check.
- What feeds it: "raw notes ... supplied" in the chat. The skill never says where they come from. What it produces: the `.md` file in the chat. Nothing is saved in a folder and nothing is sent.
- Receives from / hands over to: nobody. No other skill names it (the board's old text mentioned "client-review-notes timing across multiple completed lines" and "update client record"; no skill calls it). It works in Paul's "Summarized Client Review notes" Claude Project.

## Flaws found
Client data (S11): none.
Gaps and contradictions:
1. **No source and no access routes (S3, S4).** The raw notes are "supplied". There is no instruction to look in the client's Outlook contact through the routes (computer, Microsoft 365, Zapier), no log of what was tried, no way to know the notes are complete. "If notes appear lost or clearly incomplete, flag" is a rule nothing can check.
2. **No sample of the raw input.** The skill describes the output in detail (the example is partial: "...continue through all 18 fields...") but never shows the layout of the raw notes. A program cannot split them into entries or map a line to a field without it. Fields 6 to 14 have no definitions or allowed values ("Got details from", "Adviser Allocation", "Portfolio Review Month").
3. **"Summarise" against "zero facts dropped".** The core rule asks for condensed bullets AND for no fact lost; nothing can prove the second.
4. **"Verbatim" has no proof.** Contract numbers, DOC dates, premiums, status notes, IDs, tax numbers and commission lines "must be reproduced verbatim"; nothing compares them with the source.
5. **Not recorded, two ways.** Line 113 says `Not recorded.` (with a full stop); the example (line 245) writes `Not recorded` without one; the Medical Aid rule writes `Contract: Not recorded / not brokers yet.`
6. **The header is ambiguous.** `Firstname Lastname (Client) (ID number)`: the Categories field can be `Client / BrightRock Lead` or `Client / Non-Replacement`; which text goes in the header brackets is not said.
7. **Notes order and headings.** "Chronological order" does not say oldest first (the example is oldest first); month-only headings (**MARCH 2024**) and day headings (**14 FEBRUARY 2022**) are both shown with no rule for which to use.
8. **Duplicates only "in the current session".** A client formatted yesterday is not found today; there is no state on disk (S8). "Flag first, then merge" asks Paul nothing recorded.
9. **Delivery has no file name and no home.** "A downloadable `.md` file" only; no naming pattern (S6), no saved copy in the client's folder, no listing check.
10. **The glossary contains guesses.** "GPMR - a further portfolio/management report type", "PMA report - a policy/portfolio management/analysis report", "BSC Personalised - a BrightRock personalised cover product/report", "GCS monthly run - a recurring monthly commission/compliance run": hedged definitions nobody confirmed, in a list whose own rule is "reproduce as given, don't expand".
11. **Rules 1 and 9 and the Project pointers:** re-load the skill (a patch a program makes unnecessary), `propose_skills` (S7 replaced it), the Project changelog `client-review-notes-changelog.md`, and "in the Summarized Client Review notes project" (the skill should work in any chat).
12. **System-generated timestamps** ("timestamps that look system-generated ... flag") have no definition of what that looks like.
Nothing forces the rules (text only):
13. **The whole record is hand-built every time**: field order, bold labels, rules, three sub-headings, the policy line format, the Commission line format, the date headings. The 12-box final checklist is ticked by eye; no PASS or FAIL.
14. **Multi-part requests and "do not contact"** are marked CRITICAL but only by wording; the scope of a request (which products, by contract number) is lost silently when summarising.
15. **Typos are kept and flagged "apparent"** with no way to find them (a near-copy of a contract number, a truncated or scientific-notation ID such as 8.0E+12, an ID that is not 13 digits, a policy number in the ID place).
16. **Long reading, no lock.** Raw notes of a long-standing client can run to many entries; nothing makes Claude read them all.
Structure (S1, S5): 17. One 297-line page holds the rules, the reference lists (terminology, key contacts, broker code, systems) and the example; the changelog table and standing note are typed.

## What a program CAN and CANNOT do here (honest)
- CAN: register the raw notes (a file or pasted text) as the one source, log how they were obtained, and serve them in order with a reading lock; hold the record as ONE data file (18 fields, the three Life & Investments lists, Notes date blocks, Commission) and draw the `.md` in the fixed layout (bold labels, value below, `---`, blank lines, no sign-off, `Not recorded.` everywhere, BOLD ALL CAPS date headings in oldest-first order, no HTML); build the file name; check all 18 fields present and in order; **prove every verbatim item** (contract number, DOC date, premium, status note, ID, tax number, each commission line) appears character for character in the raw notes; **check coverage**: every number, date, rand amount, contract number, ID-like token, email, web link and Planner link in the raw notes appears in the record, or is listed as deliberately left out with a reason shown to Paul (per entry, when the raw notes carry dates, so a request's scope is not lost); raise flags it can find (a near-copy of a contract number, scientific notation, a truncated or non-13-digit ID, an ID that fails its check digit, a timestamp pattern that looks system-made); find "do not contact" and "do not call" lines by phrase search and refuse a record that carries one without the warning flag and the verbatim line; run a duplicate check against the records of this run and any earlier `.md` records you give it, show both and ask before merging, and prove nothing from either version was lost; print the checklist as a short audit.
- CANNOT: prove the summary keeps the MEANING of every sentence (a decision, who advised what, a rationale): coverage proves the figures, dates, names and links are kept, the wording stays attested; know that the Outlook notes are complete or correct; decide what is "material" or "filler"; settle a real conflict between two duplicates (it asks); know what a "system-generated" timestamp is beyond a pattern.
- Size: SMALL to MEDIUM, smaller than the last two skills (a record model with a renderer, a coverage and verbatim checker, a duplicate ledger, a step driver, a short audit, short pages, one test). One pass in this chat, no helpers.
- Catch: with no sample of the raw layout in the skill, the entry-by-entry coverage check depends on how the raw notes mark their entries. If they carry no dates, coverage is checked over the whole note instead (weaker). I need ONE invented sample of the layout from you (question 1).
