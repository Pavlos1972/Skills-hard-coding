Contents: the nine sections, Section 8 headings, standing values, totals, look.

The Summary keeps this fixed nine-section structure (Paul, 28/10/2026; the Summary carries no source box of its own, and the red Items Requiring Follow-Up section carries the important flags):
1. **Header**: title "Client Fact Find Summary", client name, advisor, date completed. For a spouse of a couple seen together, one line says it is a joint meeting with a spouse, covers this spouse only, and that debts and children are common to both.
2. **1. Client Profile**: name and surname, date of birth, age, ID or passport number, nationality, cell number, work number, personal email, work email, referral from, type of meeting, smoker, highest education, marital status.
3. **2. Occupation & Income**: occupation, business details (only for a business owner), occupational duties, income and expenses.
4. **3. Current Financial Arrangements**: Risk Cover & Retirement; Investments & Savings; Medical, Legal & Short-Term Insurance.
5. **4. Debt**: one block per debt, then the totals.
6. **5. Children & Childcare**: one block per child, then the total childcare cost. Always shown when the Fact Find has children.
7. **6. Health**.
8. **7. What the Client Requires**: Personal Risk Cover & Retirement; Investment & Insurance Needs (Business Assurance and Key Man only for a business owner). FNA, Shortfall and Reduced quote lines name the insurer and say New Business or Servicing exactly as in the Fact Find: there is no default.
9. **8. Action Plan - Cover Calculations**: the section title keeps the words "Action Plan", but its sub-headings are fixed (Paul, 15/10/2026): **Life Cover - Fractured Cover** (then Standard Life Cover), **Disability Cover - Fractured Cover** (then Standard Permanent Disability), **Dread Disease Cover** and **Income Protection / Sickness Benefit Cover**. The words "Action Plan" are never used in those sub-headings.
10. **9. Items Requiring Follow-Up**: red bullets, see the previous page.

## Standing values in Section 8 (the checker compares them with the Fact Find)
- Life Cover - Fractured Cover: Life Cover (WOL) ABI **3%**; Childcare ABI **5%**.
- Disability Cover - Fractured Cover: **no** Life Cover (WOL) ABI line; Household needs ABI **5%**; Childcare ABI **5%**.
- Dread Disease ABI **3%** always (Paul, 02/11/2026), unless Paul states otherwise for that client.
- The Dread Disease cover target is whatever the Fact Find shows (the Fact Find sets R500,000 unless there is a sourced reason).
- Standard Life Cover, Standard Permanent Disability and Income Protection ABIs and every ACI field stay as the Fact Find has them, otherwise `Pending - FNA`.

## Totals that must agree
- Section 4's totals are the sums of the debts; Section 5's total is the sum of the children's monthly costs.
- Section 8's childcare equals Section 5's total, and Section 8's debt repayments equal Section 4's total (every debt, credit life or not). Total needed adds up.
- A difference is never explained away with a footnote: the program refuses and shows both values.

## Look
A4, about 18 mm margins. Navy `#1F3A5F` titles and headings, light-blue `#EAF1F8` shaded label column in the two-column tables, 0.5 pt grey grid, follow-up flags in red `#B0271A`, no page numbers.
File name: `Fact Find Summary - DD.MM.YY - (First Surname).pdf`, derived by the program (date = the Fact Find's Date Completed, not today's date): the same shape as the Fact Find Transcript and the Follow-Up Appointment Summary Notes.
