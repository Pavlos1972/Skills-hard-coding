## Why "Not discussed" is never a default
A completed Fact Find marked a home loan's term, monthly repayment and outstanding balance, and a personal loan's outstanding balance, as "Not discussed", when all four were clearly discussed and answered on the call. The advisor caught it after delivery. Today `ff-population-transcript` has to prove every "Not discussed" before it can pass, and this skill copies the Fact Find: it never turns a value into "Not discussed" and never fills one in.

## Why debt is never left out of a total
A Fact Find Summary's Section 4 correctly totaled all four debts, but Section 8's "Total Household Income Needed" still deducted only the Fact Find's own stale total, computed before a correction and never re-derived from the corrected Section 4 figures. A first attempt to fix this excluded the home loan from Section 8 on the reasoning that its own credit life cover made it unnecessary there. Paul explicitly rejected that: it is a judgment call about insurance methodology that is his to make, not Claude's, and he does not want it made at all. Every debt is always included in full, credit life or not. The corrected version deducts the full total of all debts in Section 8, matching Section 4 exactly, with credit life left as a Section 4 fact only.

## Why the Dread Disease and Household needs ABIs are standing values
On 27 October 2026 the Fact Find had no Household needs ABI line under Permanent disability fractured. The line was added at 5% and mentioned to Paul in one line. A different figure in the Fact Find is a default, not an answer: only an ABI that Paul states explicitly for that client overrides the standing value, with the deviation and its source noted. Since 02/11/2026 the Dread Disease ABI is 3%.
