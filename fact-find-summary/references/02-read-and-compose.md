## What the program does
- **Reads** the Fact Find Transcript PDF by its fixed layout (title block, numbered sections, sub-headings, two-column tables). If the PDF is not that report (a hand-made Fact Find, an old layout, a scan) it STOPS and you tell Paul in one line. It never guesses.
- **Composes** the nine sections by fixed mapping from the Fact Find sections, row by row. Each Summary row keeps a pointer to the Fact Find row it came from, and the checker compares the two.
- **Leaves out** rows that say "Not discussed" or "N/A", and duty rows at 0%, except the always-include list (ID or passport number, cell number, work number, personal email, work email, referral from). The count is shown in the audit; the rows stay in the Fact Find. Section 8 shows every row.
- **Recomputes** the debt totals (monthly repayments, balances, asset values) and the childcare total from the item rows, to the cent.

## Items Requiring Follow-Up
Every flag of the Fact Find (differences, added standing values, anything marked with a warning sign) is carried here, quoting both values where the Fact Find does. The program adds these items from the Fact Find's own rows:
- ACI values still pending the FNA.
- Gross monthly income not confirmed.
- No will, or a will not up to date; no medical aid; no gap cover; no short-term insurance.
- No retirement funding (no retirement annuity and no pension or provident fund).
- Business ownership details incomplete (only for a business owner).
- Each document to request, the letter of authority, the next appointment and the next steps for the client.
